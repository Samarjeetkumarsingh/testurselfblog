At 1000 K, the standard Gibbs free energy of formation for titanium dioxide ($\text{TiO}_2$) reaction:

\text{Ti (s)} + \text{O}_2 \text{ (g)} \rightarrow \text{TiO}_2 \text{ (s)}, \quad \Delta G^\circ = -730,000 \text{ J/mol}

Calculate the equilibrium oxygen partial pressure ($P_{\text{O}_2}$) required for the dissociation of $\text{TiO}_2$ at $1000\text{ K}$. (Universal gas constant $R = 8.314\text{ J/mol}\cdot\text{K}$).

CORRECT ANSWER: OPTION B +2.0 Marks

Thermodynamic Derivation:
At equilibrium: ΔG° = -RT ln K = RT ln(P_O2)
ln(P_O2) = ΔG° / (RT) = -730000 / (8.314 × 1000) = -87.803
P_O2 = e^(-87.803) ≈ 7.85 × 10⁻³⁹ atm.
